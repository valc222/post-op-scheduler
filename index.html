Clinic rules

For clinic use only. Do not share link with others.
Office appointments exclude weekends and these clinic holidays: New Year’s Day, MLK Day, Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. When scheduling telehealth post op, it must be on a Wednesday evening. On Tuesdays, bottom patients should be scheduled in the morning and top patients should be scheduled in the afternoon.

Weeks 3, 4 and 6 offer optional Wednesday-evening telehealth wherever those visits exist. RFF Stage 2 / ALT Stage 3 second and fourth visits are in person only, one exact date, no alternatives. Their third visits offer alternatives and telehealth; RFF Stage 2 also has Week 6. ALT Stage 3 has no Week 6 visit. Labiaplasty’s second post-op also offers Wednesday-evening telehealth. Three-month, six-month and annual visits are in person.

Vaginoplasty, Internal Revision and Phalloplasty Stage 1: discharge surgery + 5 days; first post-op Monday strictly after discharge. If that Monday is a clinic holiday, add Friday after discharge, then Week 1 Wednesday–Friday in the holiday Monday’s week, followed by Week 2 Tuesday and the standard schedule.

Tuesday surgery dates are blocked: Tuesday is clinic day. Christmas and New Year’s close only on the actual dates, with no adjacent Friday/Monday closure. Discharge estimates are not moved for office holidays. Other fixed appointments move to the next business day with a preceding-day alternative where appropriate; RFF Stage 2 / ALT Stage 3 second and fourth visits use only the closest open business day (earlier if equally close). Holiday weekday exceptions are flagged. Week 6 is five weeks after the first-post-op week. Confirm unexpected closures before booking. Schedules are not saved after refresh.
