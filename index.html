Scheduling rules: The first post-op visit follows the surgery-specific rule. For Vaginoplasty and Internal Revision, the holiday adjustment applies only when the first post-op Monday falls on MLK Day, Presidents’ Day, Memorial Day, or Labor Day. For all later flexible visits, any appointment not specifically required on a Tuesday is interchangeable and may be scheduled on Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday; flexible visits default to Wednesday.
