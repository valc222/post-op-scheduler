Clinic rules

For clinic use only. Do not share link with others. Office appointments exclude weekends and these clinic holidays: New Year’s Day, MLK Day, Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. When scheduling a telehealth post-op, it must be on a Wednesday evening. On Tuesdays, bottom patients should be scheduled in the morning, and top patients should be scheduled in the afternoon:

Weeks 3, 4 and 6 offer optional Wednesday-evening telehealth wherever those visits exist. Exception: Phalloplasty RFF Stage 2 and ALT Stage 3 second and fourth visits must be in person; their third visits offer telehealth. All other visits, including 3-month, 6-month and annual follow-ups, are in person.

Vaginoplasty, Internal Revision and Phalloplasty Stage 1: discharge surgery + 5 days; first visit Monday strictly after discharge. If that first Monday is a clinic holiday, add Friday after discharge, then a Week 1 visit Wednesday–Friday of the holiday Monday’s week. Continue Week 2 Tuesday and standard follow-ups.

Holiday conflicts otherwise move to the next business day; the prior business day is shown as an alternative where appropriate. Weekday exceptions are labeled. Hospital discharge estimates are not moved for office holidays. Week 6 is anchored to the first-post-op week, five weeks later. Confirm one-time closures before booking. No patient identifiers required; schedules are not saved after refresh.
