Scheduling rules: The first post-op visit follows the surgery-specific rule. For Vaginoplasty and Internal Revision, the holiday adjustment applies only when the first post-op Monday falls on MLK Day, Presidents’ Day, Memorial Day, or Labor Day. For all later flexible visits, any non-Tuesday day is interchangeable: Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, or Friday. The calendar shows the default Wednesday date plus the other acceptable dates.
